\section{Dual Substrate}

Let $\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{comp}}$ be the composite substrate algebra from A6.x.

Define the dual substrate:
\[
\mathcal{A}^\star_{\mathrm{comp}} =
\mathrm{Hom}(\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{comp}}, \mathbb{R}).
\]

\textbf{Theorem (Dual Existence).}
If $\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{comp}}$ is finite-dimensional, then:
\[
\mathcal{A}^\star_{\mathrm{comp}} \text{ exists and is unique}.
\]

\section{Dual-Compatibility Operator}

\[
\mathcal{T}(p) :
\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{comp}}(p)
\to
\mathcal{A}^\star_{\mathrm{comp}}(p)
\]

\[
\mathcal{T}(p)(\mathcal{O}) = \langle \mathcal{O}, K(p) \rangle,
\]
where $K(p)$ is the kernel-preserving functional.

\textbf{Theorem (Kernel Preservation).}
\[
\ker(\mathcal{O}) = \ker(\mathcal{T}(p)(\mathcal{O})).

This formalizes Constructive Fix 13.

\[

\mathcal{T}_1 \circ \mathcal{T}_2

\mathcal{T}_2 \circ \mathcal{T}_1.

\[

This ensures dual-algebra consistency.
