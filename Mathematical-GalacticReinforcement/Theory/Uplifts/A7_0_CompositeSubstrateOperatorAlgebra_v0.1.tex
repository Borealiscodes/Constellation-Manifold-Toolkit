\section{Composite Curvature Operator}

modal projection.

\textbf{Definition (Composite Curvature Operator).}
\[
\mathcal{C}(p) = \Pi_{\mathrm{T}}(p) \cdot K_{\mathrm{RSVP}}(p).
\]

\textbf{Theorem (Continuity).}
If both $K_{\mathrm{RSVP}}$ and $\Pi_{\mathrm{T}}$ are continuous, then:
\[
\mathcal{C}(p) \text{ continuous in } p.
\]

This preserves Fix 1 and Fix 5.
