\[

\section{Gluing Condition}

Let $\{U_i\}$ be an open cover of $U$.

\textbf{Definition (Gluing).}
If $s_i \in \mathcal{C}(U_i)$ satisfy:
\[
s_i|_{U_i \cap U_j} = s_j|_{U_i \cap U_j},
\]
then there exists a unique:
\[
s \in \mathcal{C}(U)
\]
such that:
\[
s|_{U_i} = s_i.
\]

\textbf{Theorem (Semantic Gluing).}
If semantic sections agree locally, they glue globally.

This enforces Fix 12 (semantic lift preservation).
