\section{Spectral Decomposition of Resonance Adjacency Operators}

Let $A$ denote the resonance adjacency matrix associated with the ontology
manifold $\mathcal{M} = (\mathcal{O}, \mathcal{R}, \rho)$. This module defines
the spectral decomposition of $A$ and the associated stability diagnostics.

\subsection{Eigenstructure}

The spectral decomposition of $A$ is defined by:
\[
A v_k = \lambda_k v_k,
\]
where $\lambda_k$ are the eigenvalues and $v_k$ are the corresponding
eigenvectors.

Define the spectral radius:
\[
\rho(A) = \max_k |\lambda_k|.
\]

\subsection{Spectral Projection Operators}

Define the spectral projection operator for eigenvalue $\lambda_k$:
\[
P_k = v_k v_k^\top.
\]

The matrix $A$ admits the decomposition:
\[
A = \sum_k \lambda_k P_k.
\]

\subsection{Cluster Stability}

Define the cluster stability envelope:
\[
E_k = |\lambda_k|.
\]

A resonance cluster is stable when:
\[
E_k < \rho(A).
\]

A dominant cluster satisfies:
\[
E_k = \rho(A).
\]

\subsection{Spectral Collapse}

Spectral collapse occurs when:
\[
A^n \to \mathbf{1} v,
\]
where $v$ is the dominant eigenvector.

This condition implies:
\[
\lambda_{\max} \text{ is unique and } |\lambda_{\max}| \gg |\lambda_k| \text{ for } k \ne \max.
\]

\subsection{Spectral Drift}

Define spectral drift between two resonance matrices $A$ and $B$:
\[
D_{\mathrm{spec}} = \left\| \mathrm{Spec}(A) - \mathrm{Spec}(B) \right\|.
\]

Spectral drift measures deviation in cluster structure.

\subsection{Spectral Continuity}

Define spectral continuity on the manifold:
\[
\mathrm{Spec}(A(p)) \text{ continuous in } p.
\]

Spectral discontinuity indicates resonance tearing.
