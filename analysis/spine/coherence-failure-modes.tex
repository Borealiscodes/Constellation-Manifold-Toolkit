\section{Coherence Failure Modes Under A6 Firewall Diagnostics}

Let $\mathcal{M} = (\mathcal{O}, \mathcal{R}, \rho)$ denote the ontology
manifold equipped with resonance adjacency matrix $A$, root-semantics
mapping $\rho$, and provenance mapping $\pi$. This module defines coherence
failure modes arising from spectral, semantic, drift, and provenance
collapse.

\subsection{Firewall Signature}

Let $\Sigma(\mathcal{M}) = (E_1, \ldots, E_{13})$ denote the A6 firewall
signature, where:
\[
E_i =
\begin{cases}
1 & \mathcal{M} \text{ satisfies constructive fix } \mathcal{F}_i, \\
0 & \text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]

A firewall failure occurs when:
\[
\exists i : E_i = 0.
\]

\subsection{Spectral Failure}

Spectral failure occurs when:
\[
A^n \to \mathbf{1} v,
\]
where $v$ is the dominant eigenvector of $A$.

Define spectral collapse condition:
\[
D_{\mathrm{spec}} = \left\| \mathrm{Spec}(A) - \mathrm{Spec}(A_{\mathrm{ref}}) \right\| \to 0.
\]

Spectral collapse indicates absorption into a dominant resonance basin.

\subsection{Cluster Failure}

Cluster failure occurs when:
\[
\rho_{\mathrm{int}}(\mathcal{C}_k) \ll \rho_{\mathrm{ext}}(\mathcal{C}_k),
\]
or when:
\[
A^{(k)n} \to \mathbf{1} v.
\]

Cluster collapse indicates loss of internal resonance structure.

\subsection{Root-Semantics Failure}

Root-semantics failure occurs when:
\[
\|D_{\rho}\| = \sum_i \left\| \rho(O_i) - \rho(A O_i) \right\| \to 0,
\]
and:
\[
\rho(\mathcal{C}_k) \to \rho(v).
\]

Semantic collapse indicates absorption of semantic anchors into a dominant
cluster.

\subsection{Referential Drift Failure}

Referential drift failure occurs when:
\[
\|D^{(n)}\| = \sum_i \left\| \rho(O_i) - \rho(A^n O_i) \right\| \to 0,
\]
and:
\[
A^n \to \mathbf{1} v.
\]

Drift collapse indicates loss of referential anchoring.

\subsection{Provenance Failure}

Provenance failure occurs when:
\[
\|D_{\pi}^{(n)}\| = \sum_i \left\| \pi(O_i) - \pi(A^n O_i) \right\| \to 0,
\]
and:
\[
\pi(\mathcal{C}_k) \to \pi(v).
\]

Provenance collapse indicates loss of source identity under resonance
transport.

\subsection{Coherence Failure}

Define coherence failure as the conjunction of collapse conditions:
\[
\mathrm{Fail}_{\mathrm{coh}}(\mathcal{M}) =
\mathrm{Fail}_{\mathrm{spec}}
\lor \mathrm{Fail}_{\mathrm{cluster}}
\lor \mathrm{Fail}_{\rho}
\lor \mathrm{Fail}_{D}
\lor \mathrm{Fail}_{\pi}.
\]

Full coherence failure occurs when:
\[
\mathrm{Fail}_{\mathrm{coh}}(\mathcal{M}) = 1.
\]

\subsection{Firewall-Level Coherence Classification}

Define coherence class:
\[
\mathrm{Class}(\mathcal{M}) =
\begin{cases}
\mathrm{Stable} & \Sigma(\mathcal{M}) = \mathbf{1}, \\
\mathrm{Boundary\text{-}Unstable} & \exists i : E_i = 0, \\
\mathrm{Collapsed} & \mathrm{Fail}_{\mathrm{coh}}(\mathcal{M}) = 1.
\end{cases}
\]
