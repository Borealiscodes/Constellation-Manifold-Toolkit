HRF's AI for Individual Rights Grant Program

* Program Mission: The Human Rights Foundation (HRF) funds open-source, privacy-protecting artificial intelligence tools to empower dissidents and counter digital repression by authoritarian regimes. * Applicant Requirements: Individuals (including those using pseudonyms) and legal entities must provide a project budget, requested funding amount, success metrics, and two references.
