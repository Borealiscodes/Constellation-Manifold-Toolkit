HRF's AI for Individual Rights Grant Program

* Program Mission: The Human Rights Foundation (HRF) funds open-source, privacy-protecting artificial intelligence tools to empower dissidents and counter digital repression by authoritarian regimes. 
* Key Focus Areas: Supported projects include Open Source, Privacy Protecting, Bitcoin, Lightning Network, Nostr, Community Building, Education, and Translation.
* Applicant Requirements: Individuals (including those using pseudonyms) and legal entities must provide a project budget, requested funding amount, success metrics, and two references. 
* Vetting Standards: HRF conducts due diligence on publicly available materials and disqualifies applicants who support authoritarian regimes, endorse violence, or express discrimination.
