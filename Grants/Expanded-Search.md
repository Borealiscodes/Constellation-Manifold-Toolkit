# Grants Similar to HRF's AI for Individual Rights

Several organizations offer grants aligned with the Human Rights Foundation's (HRF) focus on **open-source technology, digital privacy, human rights, and decentralized protocols**. These programs support developers, researchers, and activists building tools to resist surveillance and censorship.

---

## 1. Privacy, Open-Source & Internet Freedom Grants

* **Open Technology Fund (OTF) – Internet Freedom Fund**
  * **Focus:** Open-source circumvention tools, privacy-enhancing technologies, and applied censorship research.
  * **Award Size:** $10,000 – $900,000 (for individuals and organizations globally).
  * **Link:** [Open Technology Fund](https://opentech.fund)

* **NLnet Foundation – NGI Zero Funds**
  * **Focus:** Open-source software, decentralized protocols, privacy tools, and open hardware backed by the European Commission's Next Generation Internet initiative.
  * **Award Size:** €5,000 – €50,000.
  * **Link:** [NLnet Foundation](https://nlnet.nl)

* **Digital Freedom Fund (DFF)**
  * **Focus:** Digital rights advocacy, technical research, and strategic litigation in Europe to advance human rights in digital spaces.
  * **Link:** [Digital Freedom Fund](https://digitalfreedomfund.org)

---

## 2. AI & Human Rights Grants

* **European Artificial Intelligence & Society Fund**
  * **Focus:** Civil society initiatives ensuring AI serves people and society rather than exacerbating surveillance or inequality (algorithmic accountability, public AI registries, and regulatory enforcement).
  * **Link:** [European AI & Society Fund](https://europeanaifund.org)

* **Patrick J. McGovern Foundation**
  * **Focus:** Nonprofits and research institutions using AI and data science to advance human rights, investigative journalism, and government transparency.
  * **Link:** [Patrick J. McGovern Foundation](https://mcgovern.org)

* **Free Press Unlimited – AI & Journalism Grants**
  * **Focus:** Projects combining AI tooling with investigative journalism or anti-misinformation efforts.
  * **Link:** [Free Press Unlimited](https://freepressunlimited.org)

---

## 3. Decentralized Tech, Bitcoin & Nostr Grants

* **HRF Bitcoin Development Fund (BDF)**
  * **Focus:** Open-source developers, educators, and translators working on Bitcoin, Lightning Network, Ecash, and Nostr to help dissidents bypass financial censorship.
  * **Link:** [HRF Bitcoin Development Fund](https://hrf.org)

* **OpenSats**
  * **Focus:** General project grants and long-term fellowships for open-source contributors working on Bitcoin, Nostr, and censorship-resistant communication tools.
  * **Link:** [OpenSats](https://opensats.org)

* **Btrust Grants**
  * **Focus:** Decentralizing Bitcoin and open-source software development in the Global South (particularly Africa and Latin America) through developer grants and fellowships.
  * **Link:** [Btrust](https://btrust.tech)
