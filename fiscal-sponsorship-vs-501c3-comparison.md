# Fiscal Sponsorship vs. Custom 501(c)(3): Open-Source & HRD Structural Comparison

**Overview:** Fiscal sponsorship eliminates most of the regulatory landmines associated with custom IRS Form 1023 filings—such as related-party contracting scrutiny, board independence ratios, and religious/scientific hybrid framing—while offering safer operational shelters for Human Rights Defenders (HRDs) and independent researchers working under duress.

## 2. Pathway Breakdown

### A. Open Source Collective (OSC) & Open Source Europe
* **Best For:** Lightweight, transparent open-science and open-source software tooling.
* **Mechanism:** Projects maintain a public financial ledger where contributors submit milestone invoices or expense receipts against donated funds.
* **Compliance Advantage:** Replaces custom arm's-length LLC contracts and family-board voting workarounds with third-party expense moderation. Compliance reviewers independently verify that disbursements fulfill the open-source mandate before releasing funds.
* **Trade-Off:** Every financial transaction is publicly visible by default, which maximizes open-science accountability but limits operational anonymity.

### B. Software Freedom Conservancy (SFC)
* **Best For:** Projects requiring strong intellectual property stewardship, legal defense, and open-source license enforcement.
* **Mechanism:** Uses a **Model A** comprehensive sponsorship structure where the project is legally integrated into SFC's 501(c)(3). SFC holds trademarks, copyrights, and assets in trust for the public and handles accounting and legal counsel.
* **Compliance Advantage:** Insulates individual maintainers behind SFC's corporate umbrella while providing dedicated legal capacity to enforce permissive or copyleft license terms.

### C. HRD & Digital Security Structures
* **Best For:** Independent researchers and Human Rights Defenders operating under coercive conditions or transnational repression.
* **Operational Security Risk of Form 1023:** Incorporating a U.S. 501(c)(3) creates permanent public records (state corporate registries, IRS Form 1023, annual Form 990 filings) that expose officers' legal names and physical addresses.
* **Alternative Structures:**
  * **Model C Fiscal Sponsorships (Pre-Approved Grant Relationship):** Sponsors such as Code for Science & Society, Aspiration Tech, or New Venture Fund accept institutional grants on behalf of an independent project and re-grant funds under a Model C agreement. The project retains its own intellectual property and independent identity without listing maintainers on public nonprofit board filings.
  * **Rapid-Response & Protection Funds:** Networks such as the Digital Defenders Partnership (Hivos), Access Now's Digital Security Helpline, and Front Line Defenders provide rapid-response micro-grants (often up to €5,000–€15,000) specifically designed for individuals or small teams building defensive tooling under duress, requiring no corporate wrapper.

---

## 3. Summary Takeaways

* **Proportionality:** Forming a standalone 501(c)(3) with custom religious/scientific charters and general-income grant programs creates heavy regulatory overhead (`IRC § 4958`, `IRC § 4945`, public support tests) that is disproportionate for early-stage open-source research collectives.
* **Speed & Safety:** Utilizing **Open Source Collective** (for transparent public-ledger R&D) or a **Model C / HRD fiscal sponsor** (for privacy-sensitive and duress-context work) achieves full grant eligibility within weeks while eliminating personal IRS compliance liability.
