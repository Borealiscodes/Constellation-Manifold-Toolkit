\section{Filtration Structure}
\label{sec:FiltrationStructure}

\subsection{Overview}
The Soft 80-dimensional manifold $\mathcal{M}^{80}$ is organized by a
filtration hierarchy
\[
\mathcal{F}_0 \subseteq \mathcal{F}_1 \subseteq \cdots \subseteq \mathcal{F}_{80},
\]
where each $\mathcal{F}_k$ is a definable submanifold representing altitude
level $k$. This filtration provides the structural backbone for the operator
altitude and ensures that domain operators $\mathcal{D}$ and altitude operators
$\mathcal{A}$ act coherently across layers.

The filtration is grounded in category-theoretic foundations
\citeClassA{Awodey2006,Adamek1994,BarrWells1999} and topos-theoretic
stratification \citeClassC{BarrWells2000,Bell1982}.

\subsection{Filtration Definition}
\begin{definition}[Filtration]
A filtration on $\mathcal{M}^{80}$ is a sequence of embeddings
\[
i_k : \mathcal{F}_k \hookrightarrow \mathcal{F}_{k+1}
\]
such that each $\mathcal{F}_k$ is a smooth submanifold and the embeddings are
functorial with respect to domain operators.
\end{definition}

\subsection{Functoriality}
Functoriality ensures that altitude transitions preserve definability:
\[
\mathcal{D}(i_k(p)) = i_k(\mathcal{D}(p)).
\]
This follows from accessible category structure
\citeClassA{Adamek1994}.

\subsection{Altitude Operators}
Altitude operators $\mathcal{A}$ act as filtration lifts:
\[
\mathcal{A} : \mathcal{F}_k \to \mathcal{F}_{k+1}.
\]
These operators are definable functors whose naturality squares commute:
\[
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathcal{F}_k \arrow[r,"\mathcal{A}"] \arrow[d,"i_k"']
& \mathcal{F}_{k+1} \arrow[d,"i_{k+1}"] \\
\mathcal{F}_{k+1} \arrow[r,"\mathcal{A}"']
& \mathcal{F}_{k+2}
\end{tikzcd}
\]

\subsection{Sheaf-Like Behavior}
Topos-theoretic foundations \citeClassC{BarrWells2000,Bell1982} ensure that
filtration layers behave sheaf-like over the base manifold:
\[
\mathcal{F}_k(U \cup V) = \mathcal{F}_k(U) \cup \mathcal{F}_k(V).
\]

\subsection{Compatibility with Operator Algebra}
Filtration layers provide the domain structure for the operator algebra:
\[
\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{comp}}
=
\langle
\Phi_5, \Phi_7, \Phi_8, \Phi_8^t, \Phi_9,
\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{A}, \mathcal{C}, \mathcal{P}
\rangle.
\]
Domain operators $\mathcal{D}$ and altitude operators $\mathcal{A}$ are
filtration-preserving by construction.

\subsection{Summary}
The filtration hierarchy defines the altitude structure of $\mathcal{M}^{80}$,
provides the categorical backbone for operator action, and ensures compatibility
with the Soft 80D manifold geometry and the A7 operator altitude.
