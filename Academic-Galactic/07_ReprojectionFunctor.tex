\section{Reprojection Functor}
\label{sec:ReprojectionFunctor}

\subsection{Overview}
The reprojection functor restores geometric consistency across filtration
layers, fiber bundles, spectral bundles, and collapse surfaces of the Soft
80-dimensional manifold $\mathcal{M}^{80}$. It is denoted:
\[
\mathcal{R} : \mathcal{F}_k \to \mathcal{F}_k,
\]
and acts as an idempotent geometric correction operator.

The reprojection functor interacts with:
\begin{itemize}
    \item domain operator $\mathcal{D}$ (Module 01),
    \item altitude operator $\mathcal{A}$ (Module 01),
    \item curvature envelopes $\Phi_7$ (Module 02),
    \item holonomy transport $\Phi_8$ (Module 02),
    \item drift operator $\Phi_8^t$ (Module 02),
    \item spectral operators $\Phi_5$ (Module 03),
    \item collapse surfaces $C_k$ (Module 04),
    \item dual operator $\mathcal{C}$ (Module 06),
    \item completion operator $\Phi_9$ (Module 08).
\end{itemize}

Functorial foundations follow categorical-homotopical operator algebra
\citeClassD{EmmersonBuchanan2025}.

\subsection{Idempotence}
\begin{definition}[Idempotent Reprojection]
A reprojection operator is idempotent if:
\[
\mathcal{R}^2 = \mathcal{R}.
\]
\end{definition}

Idempotence ensures that repeated reprojection does not alter geometric
structure beyond the first correction.

\subsection{Geometric Consistency Restoration}
Reprojection restores geometric consistency by correcting drift-induced
topology modulation:
\[
\mathcal{R}(\Phi_8^t(p)) = \mathcal{R}(p).
\]

This ensures stability under:
\begin{itemize}
    \item collapse-surface evolution (Module 04),
    \item holonomy-induced deformation (Module 02),
    \item spectral curvature variation (Module 03).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Functoriality}
Reprojection is functorial with respect to domain and altitude operators:
\[
\mathcal{R}(\mathcal{D}(p)) = \mathcal{D}(\mathcal{R}(p)),
\qquad
\mathcal{R}(\mathcal{A}(p)) = \mathcal{A}(\mathcal{R}(p)).
\]

This ensures altitude-preserving geometric correction.

\subsection{Naturality}
Reprojection satisfies naturality with respect to holonomy:
\[
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathcal{F}_k \arrow[r,"\Phi_8"] \arrow[d,"\mathcal{R}"']
& \mathcal{F}_k \arrow[d,"\mathcal{R}"] \\
\mathcal{F}_k \arrow[r,"\Phi_8"']
& \mathcal{F}_k
\end{tikzcd}
\]

Naturality ensures that holonomy transport commutes with geometric correction.

\subsection{Spectral Compatibility}
Spectral operators satisfy:
\[
\Phi_5(\mathcal{R}(p)) = \Phi_5(p),
\]
ensuring that reprojection does not alter spectral signatures.

Spectral invariants remain stable:
\[
I_{\mathrm{spec}}(\mathcal{R}(p)) = I_{\mathrm{spec}}(p).
\]

\subsection{Curvature Compatibility}
Curvature envelopes satisfy:
\[
\Phi_7(\mathcal{R}(p)) = \Phi_7(p),
\]
ensuring that reprojection preserves curvature structure.

This is consistent with:
\begin{itemize}
    \item holonomy-curvature interaction \citeClassA{BlackholeHolonomy2026},
    \item informational holonomy curvature \citeClassA{GutierrezUle2026},
    \item discrete-to-continuous curvature convergence \citeClassA{LiPengDong2026}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Dual Compatibility}
Dual operators commute with reprojection:
\[
\mathcal{C}(\mathcal{R}(p)) = \mathcal{R}(\mathcal{C}(p)).
\]

This ensures dual-stable geometric correction.

\subsection{Collapse-Surface Stability}
Reprojection stabilizes collapse surfaces:
\[
\mathcal{R}(C_k) = C_k.
\]

This ensures that collapse-surface stratification (Module 04) remains invariant
under geometric correction.

\subsection{Completion Interaction}
Completion operator satisfies:
\[
\Phi_9(\mathcal{R}(p)) = \Phi_9(p),
\]
ensuring that invariant extraction is unaffected by reprojection.

\subsection{Summary}
The reprojection functor defines idempotent geometric correction, functoriality,
naturality, spectral compatibility, curvature preservation, dual consistency,
collapse-surface stability, and completion invariance. It ensures that geometric
structure remains coherent across filtration layers and operator-induced
manifold evolution.
