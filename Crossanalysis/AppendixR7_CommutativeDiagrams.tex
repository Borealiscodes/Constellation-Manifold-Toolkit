\section*{Appendix R7: Commutative Diagrams for the NDH Operator Algebra}

\subsection*{R7.0 Overview}

This appendix provides commutative diagrams demonstrating that the NDH
interpretation operator
\[
I_p : X_{42} \to \{c\}
\]
commutes with all admissible operators in the NDH algebra, including
dimensional motion operators, field operators, tensor operators, and the
full transformation stack. These diagrams certify the fixed-point
invariance established in Appendix R6.

\subsection*{R7.1 Preliminaries}

Let $X_{42}$ be the 42-dimensional smooth manifold defined in Appendix
R4.

Let $\mathcal{O}(X_{42})$ be the class of admissible smooth operators:
\[
\mathcal{O}(X_{42}) = \{ A : X_{42} \to X_{42} \mid A \text{ smooth} \}.
\]

Let $E$ be the interpretive-field bundle defined in Appendix R5.

Let $\mathcal{I}_{\Omega} \in \Gamma(E)$ be an interpretive-field
section.

Let $T$ be the transformation stack defined in Appendix R3.

\subsection*{R7.2 Commutativity with Admissible Operators}

For any $A \in \mathcal{O}(X_{42})$, the following diagram commutes:

\[
\begin{CD}
X_{42} @>A>> X_{42} \\
@V I_p VV @VV I_p V \\
\{c\} @= \{c\}
\end{CD}
\]

\textbf{Explanation.}  
Since $I_p$ is constant:
\[
I_p(A(x)) = c = I_p(x),
\]
so the diagram commutes.

\subsection*{R7.3 Commutativity with Dimensional Motion}

For any dimensional motion operator $d_k : X_{42} \to X_{42}$:

\[
\begin{CD}
X_{42} @>d_k>> X_{42} \\
@V I_p VV @VV I_p V \\
\{c\} @= \{c\}
\end{CD}
\]

\textbf{Explanation.}  
Dimensional motion does not affect interpretation:
\[
I_p(d_k(x)) = I_p(x).
\]

\subsection*{R7.4 Commutativity with Field Operators}

For orientation, frame, context, and semantic operators:
\[
\mathcal{R}_{\theta},\;
\mathcal{F}_{\Theta},\;
\mathcal{E}_{\Phi},\;
\mathcal{C}_{\Psi},\;
\mathcal{S}_{\Xi} : X_{42} \to X_{42},
\]
each diagram commutes:

\[
\begin{CD}
X_{42} @>A>> X_{42} \\
@V I_p VV @VV I_p V \\
\{c\} @= \{c\}
\end{CD}
\]
where $A$ is any of the above operators.

\textbf{Explanation.}  
Field transformations preserve interpretive identity:
\[
I_p(A(x)) = I_p(x).
\]

\subsection*{R7.5 Commutativity with Tensor Operators}

For any tensor operator $A$:
\[
I_p \otimes A : X_{42} \to \{c\},
\]
the diagram:

\[
\begin{CD}
X_{42} @>A>> X_{42} \\
@V I_p VV @VV I_p V \\
\{c\} @= \{c\}
\end{CD}
\]

commutes because:
\[
(I_p \otimes A)(x) = I_p(x).
\]

\subsection*{R7.6 Commutativity with Manifold Integration}

For the manifold integration operator:
\[
I_{\mathrm{man}} : X_{42} \to X_{42},
\]
the diagram:

\[
\begin{CD}
X_{42} @>I_{\mathrm{man}}>> X_{42} \\
@V I_p VV @VV I_p V \\
\{c\} @= \{c\}
\end{CD}
\]

commutes because:
\[
I_p(I_{\mathrm{man}}(x)) = I_p(x).
\]

\subsection*{R7.7 Commutativity with the Transformation Stack}

For the full transformation stack:
\[
T = 
\mathcal{S}_{\Xi} \circ
\mathcal{C}_{\Psi} \circ
\mathcal{E}_{\Phi} \circ
\mathcal{F}_{\Theta} \circ
\mathcal{R}_{\theta} \circ
I_{\mathrm{man}},
\]
the diagram:

\[
\begin{CD}
X_{42} @>T>> X_{42} \\
@V I_p VV @VV I_p V \\
\{c\} @= \{c\}
\end{CD}
\]

commutes because each operator in the stack commutes with $I_p$.

\subsection*{R7.8 Commutativity with Interpretive Fields}

For interpretive-field sections:
\[
\mathcal{I}_{\Omega} : X_{42} \to E,
\]
the diagram:

\[
\begin{CD}
X_{42} @>T>> X_{42} @>\mathcal{I}_{\Omega}>> E \\
@V I_p VV @. @VV I_p V \\
\{c\} @= \{c\} @>>> \{c\}
\end{CD}
\]

commutes because:
\[
I_p(\mathcal{I}_{\Omega}(T(x))) = I_p(x).
\]

\subsection*{R7.9 Summary}

All admissible NDH operators commute with the interpretation operator
$I_p$. The full operator algebra collapses to the fixed point $c$ under
interpretation. These commutative diagrams certify the fixed-point
invariance proven in Appendix R6.

This completes Appendix R7.
