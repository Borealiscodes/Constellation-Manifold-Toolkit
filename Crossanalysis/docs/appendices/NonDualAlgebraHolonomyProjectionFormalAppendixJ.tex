\subsection*{J.1 Non-Dual Algebra}

Let $X$ be a Hilbert space.

Define the non-dual algebra:
\[
\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{nd}}(X)
\]
with binary operation $\star$ satisfying:
\[
T_1 \star T_2 \neq T_2 \star T_1,
\]
\[
T_1 \star T_2 \neq T_1 T_2,
\]
\[
(T_1 \star T_2)^\dagger \neq T_2^\dagger \star T_1^\dagger.
\]

Thus $\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{nd}}$ is:
\begin{itemize}
    \item non-commutative,
    \item non-associative,
    \item non-dual (adjoint does not reverse order),
    \item holonomy-indexed.
\end{itemize}
