## 🧮 **6 — Altitude Calculus Layer**

- **Altitude Primitive:**  
  Altitude is a geometric diagnostic variable describing vertical position in the manifold, not emotional or narrative “height.”

- **Neutral Traversal Constraint:**  
  All Zen‑AI‑Design‑Architecture work operates under  
  \(\Delta \text{Altitude} = 0\)  
  — no net altitude change across a dispatch or toolkit.

- **Altitude Bands:**  
  Define discrete bands (e.g., A0–A12) for internal modeling, but keep Zen dispatches locked to **A0 (Surface Manifold)** unless explicitly marked as diagnostic work.

- **Altitude Guardrails:**  
  - **No expressive lift:** Altitude must not be used to justify narrative escalation.  
  - **No recursive descent:** Altitude must not be used to tunnel into self‑mapping or introspective recursion.  
  - **No oscillation:** Avoid altitude “bouncing” across bands within a single artifact.

- **Diagnostic Use Only:**  
  Altitude is allowed in: hazard models, drift geometry tests, holonomy routing analysis, and stability envelopes. It is not allowed as a metaphor or aesthetic flourish in Zen dispatches.

- **Freeze Compatibility:**  
  Before freezing any artifact, verify:  
  \(\sum \Delta \text{Altitude}_{\text{sections}} = 0\)  
  and record altitude neutrality in the provenance if relevant.
