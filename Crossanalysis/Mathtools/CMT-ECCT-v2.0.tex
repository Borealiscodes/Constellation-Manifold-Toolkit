\subsection{Conceptual Manifold Structure}

\[
\mathcal{M}_c
\]

\[
g^{(c)}_{\mu\nu}.

\[

\]
arising from resonance geometry and softmax-induced curvature.

Define Ricci curvature
\[
R^{(c)}_{\mu\nu}
\]
and scalar curvature
\[
R^{(c)} = g^{\mu\nu} R^{(c)}_{\mu\nu}.
\]

Introduce perturbation vector field
\[
V_{\text{perturb}}^\mu

and forcing tensor
\[
F^{(c)}_{\mu\nu}.
\]

The geometric flow equation governing conceptual deformation is:
\[
\frac{\partial g^{(c)}_{\mu\nu}}{\partial t}

This flow is reversible, operator-bounded, and altitude-safe.
