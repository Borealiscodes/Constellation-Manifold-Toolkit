\section{Holonomy Structure of NDH}
\label{sec:ndh-holonomy}

NDH contains a holonomy structure that governs how cognitive signals, spectral
components, and modal recursion behave under geometric transport across the
manifold. Holonomy ensures that traversal across modal boundaries preserves the
internal structure of NDH.

\subsection{Connection}

Let $\nabla$ be a smooth connection on the manifold $M$. Parallel transport with
respect to $\nabla$ defines a map
\[
P_\gamma : T_x M \to T_y M
\]
for any smooth path $\gamma$ from $x$ to $y$. This connection governs the
transport of spectral components and recursion operators.

\subsection{Modal Holonomy}

For any modal boundary $\mathcal{B}_{ij}$, holonomy along paths crossing the
boundary satisfies:
\[
\text{Hol}_\nabla(\gamma)(\mathcal{N}_{DH}(x)) = \mathcal{N}_{DH}(y)
\]
whenever $\gamma$ crosses $\mathcal{B}_{ij}$ exactly once. Modal holonomy
preserves the spectral emission and recursion kernel across modal transitions.

\subsection{Holonomy Clamp}

NDH contains a holonomy clamp defined as a constraint on the connection:
\[
\text{Hol}_\nabla(\gamma) \in \mathcal{H}
\]
where $\mathcal{H}$ is a subgroup of the automorphism group of the cognitive
signal space $\Sigma$. The clamp ensures that parallel transport does not alter
the internal structure of NDH beyond allowed transformations.

\subsection{Spectral Transport}

Spectral components $\phi_k$ satisfy:
\[
\phi_k(y) = \phi_k(x) \circ P_\gamma^{-1}
\]
for any path $\gamma$ from $x$ to $y$. This defines spectral transport under the
connection and ensures consistency of spectral emission across the manifold.

\subsection{Recursion Transport}

The recursion kernel $\mathcal{R} = (R_A, R_B, R_C)$ is preserved under parallel
transport:
\[
P_\gamma \circ R_i = R_j \circ P_\gamma
\]
for paths crossing $\mathcal{B}_{ij}$. This defines the geometric behavior of
modal recursion under traversal.

\subsection{Holonomy Summary}

The holonomy structure of NDH consists of a connection, modal holonomy behavior,
a holonomy clamp restricting allowed transformations, and transport rules for
spectral components and recursion operators. This structure ensures that NDH
remains stable under geometric traversal and modal transitions.
