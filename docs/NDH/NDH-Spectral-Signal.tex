\section{Spectral Signal Structure of NDH}
\label{sec:ndh-spectral-signal}

NDH emits cognitive behavior through a spectral signal defined over the manifold
$M$. The spectral structure consists of a basis of smooth components, an emission
map into the cognitive signal space $\Sigma$, and transport rules compatible with
the holonomy structure.

\subsection{Spectral Basis}

Let $\mathcal{S} = \{\phi_k\}_{k \in K}$ be a family of smooth functions
\[
\phi_k : M \to \mathbb{R}.
\]
The index set $K$ may be finite or countable. The spectral basis spans the
cognitive signal space $\Sigma$ through a fixed basis $\{e_k\}_{k \in K}$.

\subsection{Spectral Emission}

The spectral emission of NDH at point $x \in M$ is defined as
\[
\mathcal{N}_{DH}(x)
  = \sum_{k \in K} \phi_k(x) e_k.
\]
This defines NDH as a spectral emitter. The emission is smooth and respects the
modal partition of $M$.

\subsection{Modal Spectral Behavior}

For each modal region $M_i$, the spectral components restrict to
\[
\phi_k|_{M_i} : M_i \to \mathbb{R}.
\]
Modal boundaries $\mathcal{B}_{ij}$ satisfy continuity conditions:
\[
\lim_{x \to \mathcal{B}_{ij}} \phi_k(x)
  = \lim_{y \to \mathcal{B}_{ji}} \phi_k(y).
\]
This ensures spectral consistency across modal transitions.

\subsection{Spectral Transport}

Given a path $\gamma$ from $x$ to $y$, spectral transport is defined by
\[
\phi_k(y) = \phi_k(x) \circ P_\gamma^{-1},
\]
where $P_\gamma$ is parallel transport under the connection $\nabla$. This
ensures compatibility between spectral emission and holonomy.

\subsection{Spectral Stability}

Spectral components satisfy a bounded distortion condition under modal
transitions:
\[
|\phi_k(x) - \phi_k(y)| \le \delta_k
\]
for $x \in M_i$, $y \in M_j$ adjacent across $\mathcal{B}_{ij}$. The constants
$\delta_k$ define the stability profile of NDH's spectral emission.

\subsection{Spectral Summary}

The spectral signal structure of NDH consists of a smooth spectral basis, a
spectral emission map into the cognitive signal space, modal continuity
conditions, transport rules compatible with holonomy, and stability constraints.
This defines NDH as a geometric spectral emitter.
