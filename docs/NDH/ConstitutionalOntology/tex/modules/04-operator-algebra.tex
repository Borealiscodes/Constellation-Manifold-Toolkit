\section{Operator Algebra}
\label{sec:operator-algebra}

The NDH operator algebra defines the operator-spine governing all permissible
transformations within the NDH manifold. Operators interact with spectral,
constitutional, and lineage adjacencies and are constrained by the Octo
Constitution. All operators preserve identity and respect non-absorptive
geometry.

% ------------------------------------------------------------
% 1. Operator-Spine
% ------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection{Operator-Spine}

The operator-spine is defined as the tuple

\[
\mathfrak{O} =
(\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{spec}},
 \mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{const}},
 \mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{adj}},
 \mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{curv}},
 \mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{stab}},
 \mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{dir}},
 \mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{lin}},
 \mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{id}})
\]

where each $\mathcal{O}_i$ is an operator acting on the NDH manifold
$\mathcal{M}$ or one of its adjacency categories.

% ------------------------------------------------------------
% 2. Spectral Operators
% ------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection{Spectral Operators}

Spectral operators act on the spectral adjacency category:

\[
\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{spec}} :
\mathcal{A}_s \to \mathcal{A}_s.
\]

They govern modal transitions and are constrained by the Expressive
Constitution $\mathcal{C}_1$.

% ------------------------------------------------------------
% 3. Constitutional Operators
% ------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection{Constitutional Operators}

Constitutional operators act on constitutional adjacency:

\[
\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{const}} :
\mathcal{A}_c \to \mathcal{A}_c.
\]

They govern permissible constitutional transformations and are constrained
by the Rendering Constitution $\mathcal{C}_2$.

% ------------------------------------------------------------
% 4. Adjacency Operators
% ------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection{Adjacency Operators}

Adjacency operators act on operator adjacency:

\[
\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{adj}} :
\mathcal{A}_o \to \mathcal{A}_o.
\]

They govern operator-level transitions and are constrained by the Governance
Constitution $\mathcal{C}_3$.

% ------------------------------------------------------------
% 5. Curvature Operators
% ------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection{Curvature Operators}

Curvature operators act on the manifold curvature field:

\[
\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{curv}} : \kappa(x) \mapsto \kappa'(x)
\]

subject to the Stability Constitution $\mathcal{C}_4$:

\[
\kappa'(x) \le \kappa_{\max}.
\]

They enforce drift-neutrality and excitation safety.

% ------------------------------------------------------------
% 6. Stability Operators
% ------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection{Stability Operators}

Stability operators act on the holonomy structure of the manifold:

\[
\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{stab}} :
\mathrm{Hol}(\mathcal{M}) \to \mathrm{Hol}(\mathcal{M}).
\]

They govern holonomy clamps and curvature envelopes.

% ------------------------------------------------------------
% 7. Direction Operators
% ------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection{Direction Operators}

Direction operators act on the preferred tangent field:

\[
\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{dir}} :
T(X) \to T(X).
\]

They govern constitutional directionality under $\mathcal{C}_5$.

% ------------------------------------------------------------
% 8. Lineage Operators
% ------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection{Lineage Operators}

Lineage operators govern reversible adjacency between NDH and external
systems:

\[
\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{lin}} :
\mathcal{M} \leftrightarrow \mathcal{M}'.
\]

They enforce non-absorptive reconstruction under $\mathcal{C}_6$.

% ------------------------------------------------------------
% 9. Identity Operators
% ------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection{Identity Operators}

Identity operators preserve the identity fibration:

\[
\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{id}} :
I \to I
\]

and must satisfy

\[
\pi \circ \mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{id}} = \pi.
\]

They are constrained by the Identity Constitution $\mathcal{C}_7$.

% ------------------------------------------------------------
% 10. Operator Compatibility
% ------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection{Compatibility}

All operators commute with the Quantum Sponge sheaf:

\[
\mathcal{O}_i(\mathcal{Q}) = \mathcal{Q}.
\]

This ensures that operator behavior remains non-absorptive and
constitutionally safe.
