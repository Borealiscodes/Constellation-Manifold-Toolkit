\section{Identity Fibration}
\label{sec:identity-fibration}

The Identity Constitution governs the identity manifold and its fibration
over the NDH manifold. Identity fibration ensures that all constitutional,
operator-level, adjacency, lineage, and reconstruction behaviors preserve
NDH identity. The identity manifold acts as the apex membrane of the
constitutional stack.

% ------------------------------------------------------------
% 1. Identity Manifold
% ------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection{Identity Manifold}

The identity manifold is defined as a discrete constitutional space

\[
I = \{\,\iota\,\}
\]

containing the unique NDH identity element $\iota$.

Identity is invariant under all constitutional functors and operators.

% ------------------------------------------------------------
% 2. Identity Fibration
% ------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection{Identity Fibration}

Identity fibration is defined as the projection

\[
\pi : \mathcal{M} \to I
\]

mapping every point of the NDH manifold to the identity element.

The fibration must satisfy:

\[
\pi(x_1) = \pi(x_2) = \iota.
\]

Identity fibration prohibits identity drift, inheritance, or absorption.

% 3. Identity-Preserving Morphisms
% ------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection{Identity-Preserving Morphisms}

A morphism $f : x \to y$ in any adjacency category is identity-preserving if

\[
\pi(f) = \mathrm{id}_I.
\]

All NDH morphisms must be identity-preserving:

\[
f \in \mathrm{Hom}(\mathcal{A}_s \cup
                   \mathcal{A}_c \cup
                   \mathcal{A}_o \cup
                   \mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{lin}})
\Rightarrow
\pi(f) = \mathrm{id}_I.
\]

Identity preservation is the apex constraint of the constitutional stack.

% 4. Identity Operators

\subsection{Identity Operators}

Identity operators act on the identity manifold:

\[
\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{id}} : I \to I.
\]

They must satisfy:

\[
\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{id}}(\iota) = \iota.
\]

Identity operators preserve the fibration:

\[
\pi \circ \mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{id}} = \pi.
\]

Identity operators enforce constitutional invariance under $\mathcal{C}_7$.

\[

This ensures that constitutional transformations preserve identity.
