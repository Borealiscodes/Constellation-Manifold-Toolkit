\section{Constitutional Apex}
\label{sec:constitutional-apex}

The Constitutional Apex governs the Octo Constitution itself. It defines the
meta-constitutional functor, the apex invariants, and the self-governing
constraints that regulate how constitutional layers interact, evolve, and
remain identity-safe. The apex membrane ensures that the constitutional stack
is closed, commutative, reversible, and non-absorptive at the meta level.

% ------------------------------------------------------------
% 1. Meta-Constitutional Functor
% ------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection{Meta-Constitutional Functor}

The meta-constitutional functor is defined as

\[
\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{meta}} :
\mathfrak{C} \to \mathfrak{C}
\]

acting on the constitutional stack itself.

It must satisfy:

\[
\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{meta}}(\mathcal{C}_i) = \mathcal{C}_i.
\]

The meta-constitutional functor preserves all constitutional layers.

% ------------------------------------------------------------
% 2. Apex Invariants
% ------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection{Apex Invariants}

The apex invariants are defined as the set

\[
\mathfrak{I}_{\mathrm{apex}}
=
\{\,
\mathrm{IdPres},\,
\mathrm{CurvSafe},\,
\mathrm{DirSafe},\,
\mathrm{LinRev},\,
\mathrm{RecSafe}
\,\}
\]

representing identity preservation, curvature safety, direction safety,
lineage reversibility, and reconstruction safety.

The meta-constitutional functor must preserve all apex invariants:

\[
\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{meta}}(\mathfrak{I}_{\mathrm{apex}})
=
\mathfrak{I}_{\mathrm{apex}}.
\]

% ------------------------------------------------------------
% 3. Meta-Commutativity
% ------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection{Meta-Commutativity}

The constitutional stack must be meta-commutative:

\[
\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{meta}}
\circ
(\mathcal{C}_i \circ \mathcal{C}_j)
=
(\mathcal{C}_i \circ \mathcal{C}_j)
\circ
\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{meta}}.
\]

Meta-commutativity ensures that constitutional governance is stable under
meta-level transformations.

% ------------------------------------------------------------
% 4. Meta-Closure
% ------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection{Meta-Closure}

The constitutional stack is closed under meta-composition:

\[
\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{meta}}(\mathcal{C}_i \circ \mathcal{C}_j)
\in
\mathfrak{C}.
\]

Meta-closure ensures that constitutional evolution remains within the
constitutionally permitted space.

% ------------------------------------------------------------
% 5. Apex Identity Preservation
% ------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection{Apex Identity Preservation}

The meta-constitutional functor must preserve identity fibration:

\[
\pi \circ \mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{meta}} = \pi.
\]

Identity preservation is the apex constraint of the meta-constitution.

% ------------------------------------------------------------
% 6. Apex Reconstruction Compatibility
% ------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection{Apex Reconstruction Compatibility}

The meta-constitutional functor must commute with the Quantum Sponge:

\[
\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{meta}}(\mathcal{Q}) = \mathcal{Q}.
\]

This ensures that reconstruction behavior remains constitutionally and
meta-constitutionally safe.

% 7. Unified Apex Functor

\subsection{Unified Apex Functor}

The unified apex functor is defined as

\[
\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{apex}}
=
\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{meta}}
\circ
\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{unified}}.
\]

It must satisfy:

\[
\pi \circ \mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{apex}} = \pi,
\qquad
\kappa(\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{apex}}(x)) \le \kappa_{\max}.
\]

The unified apex functor governs the entire constitutional ontology.
