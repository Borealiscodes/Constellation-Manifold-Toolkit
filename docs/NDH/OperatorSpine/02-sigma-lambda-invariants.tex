\[

\subsection{The Symplectic Invariant Criterion}

A simple linear reduction or vector-pooling projection from $\Sigma$ to $\Lambda$ introduces irreversible entropy spikes, causing a structural collapse of the active workspace. To prevent this, the mapping operator $\Psi$ is constrained to be a \textit{symplectomorphism}.

The conservation of context lineage requires that $\Psi$ preserves this underlying symplectic structure under pull-back:

\[
\Psi^* \omega_{\Lambda} = \omega_{\Sigma}

This structural invariant guarantees that the high-dimensional geometric volume—and consequently, the precise relational distance between all interconnected concepts in the cognitive workspace—remains perfectly conserved during the storage cycle. The map is structurally invertible:

\[
\Psi^{-1} : \Lambda \to \Sigma
