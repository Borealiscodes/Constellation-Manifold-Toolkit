🧩 Tile 1 — Phase 9: Projection‑Seed Geometry

What Phase 9 Does
Phase 9 builds the first geometric seed that later routing layers use.  
It’s the “starter geometry” for projection.

Inputs
- curvature energy (Phase 6)  
- spectral coupling (Phase 6)  
- collapse activation (Phase 8)  

Outputs
- projectionseedenergy  
- projectionseedcurvature  
- projectionseedbinding

Bill Nye Tile Explanation
Think of Phase 9 as making the first little geometric nugget that tells the system:  
“Here’s the shape of the projection you’ll eventually route through.”

It doesn’t route anything yet.  
It just builds the seed.

🧩 Tile 2 — Phase 10: Collapse‑Surface Ecology

What Phase 10 Does
Phase 10 turns collapse‑surface activation into ecology: scalar environmental conditions.

Inputs
- collapse activation (Phase 8)  
- projection‑seed geometry (Phase 9)  
- curvature + spectral fields (Phase 6)

Outputs
- collapseecologyenergy  
- collapseecologycurvature  
- collapseecologybinding

Bill Nye Tile Explanation
Phase 10 is like measuring the “environment” around the collapse surface.  
Not the collapse itself — the ecology around it.

It produces scalar conditions that later phases use to decide how routing should behave.

🧩 Tile 3 — Phase 11: Projection‑Routing Ecology

What Phase 11 Does
Phase 11 takes the ecology from Phase 10 and turns it into directional routing ecology.

Inputs
- collapse ecology (Phase 10)  
- projection‑seed geometry (Phase 9)  
- collapse activation (Phase 8)  
- curvature + spectral fields (Phase 6)

Outputs
- routingecologyvector_x  
- routingecologyvector_y  
- routingecologystrength

Bill Nye Tile Explanation
Phase 11 is where the system says:

“Okay, we know the environment.  
Now let’s figure out which direction routing wants to go.”

It’s the first time the system produces vectors instead of scalars.  
This is the altitude where ecology becomes directional.

---

🧩 Tile 4 — Phase 12: Projection‑Holonomy Routing

What Phase 12 Does
Phase 12 takes routing‑ecology vectors and aligns them with holonomy — the curvature‑driven transport structure.

Inputs
- routing ecology (Phase 11)  
- collapse ecology (Phase 10)  
- projection‑seed geometry (Phase 9)  
- collapse activation (Phase 8)  
- curvature + spectral fields (Phase 6)

Outputs
- projectionholonomyvector_x  
- projectionholonomyvector_y  
- projectionholonomystrength

Bill Nye Tile Explanation
Phase 12 is where routing becomes smart.

It doesn’t just point in a direction — it aligns with the holonomy of the system, meaning:

- curvature  
- spectral coupling  
- collapse geometry  
- projection geometry  

All influence the routing direction.

This is the first altitude where routing becomes transport‑grade.

🧩 Tile 5 — Phase 12+: Projection‑Collapse Routing (Preview)

What Phase 12+ Will Do
Phase 12+ (Phase 13) takes holonomy‑aligned routing and merges it with collapse geometry to produce projection‑collapse routing.

Inputs
- holonomy routing (Phase 12)  
- collapse ecology (Phase 10)  
- projection‑seed geometry (Phase 9)

Outputs
- projection‑collapse routing fields (Phase 13)

Bill Nye Tile Explanation
Phase 12+ is where routing finally interacts with the collapse surface itself.  
It’s the altitude where routing stops being “in the environment” and starts being “on the collapse geometry.”

This is the altitude where projection and collapse meet.
